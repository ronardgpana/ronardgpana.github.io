Ronard G. Paña

Hello! I am an Assistant Professor at the Visayas State University in Baybay, Leyte, Philippines, and currently a Ph.D. candidate in Energy Engineering at the University of the Philippines Diliman.

My research interests include the modeling of energy and cooling systems and the application of artificial intelligence, particularly reinforcement learning, to energy systems. I have published journal articles and conference proceedings, and have presented my work at various international conferences.
